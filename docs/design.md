# Identidade visual do MedSync

Direção: interface clara e sóbria, azul profundo e verde, navegação por áreas de trabalho, superfícies leves e tipografia de sistema sem dependência de fontes externas. Imagens editoriais ficam nas páginas de apresentação, acesso, clínica e resumos dos portais. Tabelas e prontuários priorizam o conteúdo operacional.

## Imagens

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada image_gen. São cenas ilustrativas sintéticas; não representam clientes, instalações ou pacientes reais. Os arquivos finais estão no próprio projeto e não dependem dos originais da sessão:

- `public/images/clinic-editorial.png`: apresentação pública e fundo da tela de acesso.
- `public/images/clinic-interior.png`: Minha clínica e resumo dos portais.

Prompt 1:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium healthcare SaaS website hero and authentication background photo for MedSync, a Brazilian clinical management app. Create one high-resolution wide landscape editorial photograph, aspect ratio 3:2. A contemporary welcoming Brazilian outpatient clinic with a female doctor aged about 35 in an off-white coat with stethoscope, and a female clinic administrator aged about 40 in a muted sage blouse, reviewing a laptop together at a pale oak reception desk. Natural candid expressions and relaxed posture, believable professional interaction, not looking at camera. Interior has sage green details, warm white walls, large windows with soft natural morning light, one leafy plant, clean architectural lines. Composition: people and laptop on right half, quiet uncluttered architectural breathing room on left; portrait crop of right half must also work. Warm understated color grade, muted forest green, ivory, wood, premium Scandinavian editorial photography, realistic skin texture and hands. No text, no logos, no watermark, no readable content on screen, no identifiable patients or medical records. Friendly and credible, no exaggerated advertising smiles.

Prompt 2:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape banner photograph for MedSync healthcare SaaS internal clinic overview page. Create a wide 3:2 professional editorial architectural photograph of an empty contemporary outpatient clinic reception in Brazil. Warm ivory walls, muted sage green upholstered chairs, pale natural oak reception counter, elegant indoor plant, generous indirect daylight through large windows. Quiet, welcoming, clean, human and understated, realistic architectural photography, no people, no text, no logos, no signage, no watermark, no screens or patient data. Eye-level perspective with strong clean geometry and harmonious composition; works cropped as wide short banner. Natural neutral warm color grade, premium high-end but achievable clinic interior.
